Vice Cover Studio turns your photo into a sun-soaked, multi-panel poster in the style of GTA 6 cover art: upload up to three photos (or use the built-in samples), pick one of four layouts — Hero, Triptych, Duo split or Stack — crop and position each panel, write a title and tagline, choose a sunset, neon, palm or noir grade and a lettering style, then export a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs offline from static files in your browser; photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080 in a desktop browser.
